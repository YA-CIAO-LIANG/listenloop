# ListenLoop: master one English clip a week, and hear every word

**An English listening course for students in Taiwan** · <https://listenloop-three.vercel.app>

## The problem

"Just listen more" is the usual advice, but learners end up getting the gist without being sure of every word, and they can't tell whether all that listening is making them better.

## The solution: a 7-day loop

| Day 1                                                                                                                   | Days 2–6                                                                                    | Day 7                                                                                |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Dictate** sentence by sentence; every word is checked, and missed words are highlighted with why they're hard to hear | **Shadow** each sentence; the browser shows which words you said (no recording is uploaded) | **Dictate again** and compare with Day 1, sentence by sentence: progress as a number |

Missed words come back 1, 3, and 7 days later as 30-second reviews.

## Highlights

- **Free official library**: daily life, travel, campus, study abroad, work, science news, and exam-style practice (TOEIC, TOEFL, IELTS, GEPT); excerpts from Blender's open movies.
- **Bilingual handouts**: key words, linking and weak forms, hard sentences in meaning groups, vocabulary and grammar, printable as PDF.
- **Make your own** (paid): paste text, have AI write a scenario, or use a Creative Commons YouTube video; it's split, translated, annotated, and voiced (US, UK, Australian, Indian English).
- **Classrooms**: teachers assign materials and see each student's Day 1 → Day 7 gain.
