# ListenLoop 5-minute product demo: script and shot list

> For interviews, judges, and class presentations. Record at 1920×1080 with the browser at 110%, on the live site (<https://listenloop-three.vercel.app>), with the interface switched to English.
> For phone shots, use Chrome DevTools' iPhone size or a real phone.

## Before recording

- [ ] Two accounts: a **learner** (Day 1 of a lesson done, Day 7 available: practice ahead or use the test mode) and an **admin**.
- [ ] The learner has a few misheard words due, and a few days of history.
- [ ] Notifications and the bookmarks bar off; light theme, switching to dark in the last shot.
- [ ] Microphone allowed (for shadowing).
- [ ] Pick the demo lessons: a ~1-minute official audio lesson (e.g. "Platform and Delay") and the Sintel film.
