## 中文

### 一句話介紹

ListenLoop 是給台灣學生的英文聽力練習網站：一段英文練 7 天，第 1 天聽寫、第 2～6 天跟讀、第 7 天再聽寫，和第 1 天比較，讓進步看得見。

### 產品簡介（100 字）

很多人英文聽得懂大意，卻不確定每個字。ListenLoop 用一週的節奏練熟一段 1～3 分鐘的英文：逐字聽寫找出漏聽的字、跟讀熟悉語速，第 7 天用同一份教材再測一次。聽錯的字會在 1、3、7 天後回來複習。官方教材免費，涵蓋生活、留學、職場與考試風格練習。

## English

### In one sentence

ListenLoop is an English listening course for students in Taiwan: practice one clip for seven days (dictation on Day 1, shadowing on Days 2–6, dictation again on Day 7) and see your progress against Day 1.

### About (100 words)

Many learners get the gist of spoken English without being sure of every word. ListenLoop gives one 1–3 minute clip a week-long rhythm: word-by-word dictation reveals what you missed, shadowing builds speed, and on Day 7 you take the same clip again. Missed words come back for review 1, 3, and 7 days later. Official lessons are free and cover daily life, studying abroad, work, and exam-style listening, with bilingual notes that explain why a phrase is hard to hear.

### Key facts (2026-11-12)

- 51 official lessons, 26 more in production (including excerpts from Blender's open movies)
- Chinese and English interface; works on phones and computers (PWA)
- Built in 20 days: 150+ pull requests and about 1,670 automated tests
